# 📋 Project Statement

## 🔴 Problem Statement

Traffic congestion, inefficient signal timing, road blockages, and delayed movement of emergency vehicles can affect the smooth flow of traffic. A system is required to monitor traffic conditions and manage junctions efficiently.

The **Smart Traffic Control System** provides a Java-based CLI solution that monitors traffic density, manages vehicles and roads, controls traffic signals, detects congestion, and provides emergency vehicle priority.

## 🎯 Scope of the Project

The project covers the simulation of traffic management across multiple junctions and roads.

The system can:

- Monitor junction and road traffic
- Manage different types of vehicles
- Calculate signal timing based on traffic density
- Give priority to emergency vehicles
- Detect traffic congestion
- Handle road blockage and reopening
- Suggest alternative routes
- Generate traffic statistics and reports
- Simulate multiple junctions using multithreading

## 👥 Target Users

- Traffic control operators
- Traffic management personnel
- Students learning traffic management systems
- Users interested in Java-based traffic simulation
